Modern social platforms optimise for content consumption rather than personal outcomes, and the goal-oriented communities that form around fitness, learning and building suffer from self-reported progress, absent deadlines and constant distraction. Enigma addresses this gap with a mobile-first platform in which communities run real, time-boxed challenges — for example "Build a startup in 30 days", "Lose 5 kg" or "Learn Python" — and each member's progress is proven with evidence that is verified first by an AI pre-screen and then, where confidence is insufficient, by a peer quorum, with administrative override where required. A defining liveness rule permits challenge posts only while a challenge is live, while ephemeral Stories and lightweight Notes remain available at all times; a progression economy of experience points, levels, streaks and badges sustains commitment across the lifecycle Draft → Announced → Live → Ending → Archived. Two pilot communities, Hackathon A (builders) and Anytime Fitness (fitness), anchor the launch.

The work follows an iterative process model — Communication, Planning, Modeling and Construction looping back through demonstration and feedback before Deployment — delivered over three planned iterations from a focused MVP to a feature-complete version. This report establishes the problem context and rationale, states the objectives, non-goals and measurable success criteria, and documents the requirement specification, system design, estimation, scheduling, risk management and testing artefacts produced for the project.
