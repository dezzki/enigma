Modern social media rewards content, not outcomes. People spend hours posting and scrolling but rarely finish the goals they set. Goal-focused communities do exist, yet they struggle with three problems: members report their own progress with no proof, goals have no enforced deadline, and busy feeds distract from the real task. Enigma is a mobile-first social app where communities run real, time-boxed challenges, such as losing five kilograms, learning Python, reading ten books or building a startup in thirty days. Every progress proof is checked first by AI and then, if needed, by peers at Level 3 or above and finally by an admin. Posts are allowed only while a challenge is Live, while Stories and Notes stay available at all times. Experience points, levels and badges reward effort. Two pilot communities, Hackathon A and Anytime Fitness, guide the design. This report covers the problem statement, the process model, requirements, design, estimation, scheduling, risk management and testing for the project.
